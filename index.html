the method

5 steps out of survival mode.

01 — awareness

Notice It, Don't Fight It

the first step isn't calming down — it's recognizing you're in a sensitized state to begin with. awareness, without judgment, is what starts to teach your body it's safe.

02 — movement

Move With Your Nervous System, Not Against It

movement should meet you where you are. if you're activated, that means starting low — walking, stretching, gentle movement — and listening to what your body can actually handle that day.

03 — sunlight

Get Daily Sunlight

morning light helps regulate your circadian rhythm and your stress response. a few minutes outside, consistently, sends your nervous system a steady signal of safety.

04 — nutrition

Eat to Support Your Nervous System

whole foods and steady blood sugar help keep your body out of a stress response. this isn't about restriction — it's about paying attention to what's actually in your food.

05 — consistency

Small Shifts, Repeated

regulation doesn't happen overnight. it happens through small behavior changes, repeated consistently, until your nervous system learns safety is the new normal.
